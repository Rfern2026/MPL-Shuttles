2026 FNG NA Annual MP&L Leadership Conference · Troy, 9/22–23

1 · Build Passenger List

Paste one attendee per line. Add the last 4 digits of their phone after a comma to enable reservation verification (optional — you can also fill it in per-row below later). Boarding is first come, first served — nobody is pre-assigned to a shuttle; drivers just log which shuttle each person actually gets on.

2 · Passenger List

Everyone below gets one QR boarding pass. Which shuttle someone boards is handled entirely through the Reservations tab now — passengers pick it themselves on the reservation page. The phone field is optional contact info, not currently used for verification.

3 · Drivers

Add each driver and the shuttle they're assigned to. On the Check-In tab, drivers pick their own name and the shuttle fills in automatically. Reassign anyone here anytime — changes apply immediately everywhere.

3b · Driver Substitutions

Only needed for exceptions. Each shuttle normally uses its assigned driver from above — set a substitute here when someone else covers a specific trip. Tip: add fill-in drivers with shuttle set to “Substitute only” so they appear on the trips you assign here and nowhere else. Leave everything on “(regular driver)” unless there's an actual swap.

4 · Shuttle Schedule & Capacity

Check which shuttles actually run each trip, then set a departure time and total seat capacity (including the driver's seat) for each one. A 15-seat van holds 14 passengers + driver, so enter 15. Only shuttles checked here, with a time filled in, show up on the passenger reservation page.

Clear Check-Ins

Clears everyone's boarded status across all trips — useful while testing. Leaves the passenger list, drivers, shuttle schedule, and reservations untouched.

Clear Reservations

Clears everyone's reservations and unlocks the reservation page for all passengers again — useful while testing. Leaves the passenger list, drivers, shuttle schedule, and check-ins untouched.

Reset

Clears the passenger list, drivers, shuttle schedule, reservations, and all check-ins for everyone on this event. Use between events, not during one.

Reservation Deadline

Once this date passes, the reservation site shows "Reservations Closed" and tells people to contact you directly, instead of the normal booking flow. Leave blank to never auto-close reservations.
